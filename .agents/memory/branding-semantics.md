---
name: Branding and white-label semantics
description: The legacy white-label toggle does not disable saved brand identity and palette.
---

Saving a brand name, palette, font picks or legal footer applies those settings to public readers. The legacy white-label toggle separately controls removal of Zipy platform branding.

**Why:** Interpreting the legacy toggle as a master theme on/off switch caused successful saves to have no visible effect when the user retained Zipy branding.

**How to apply:** Keep theme application separate from platform-brand removal. Verify both toggle states when changing the branding-to-public-reader flow.
