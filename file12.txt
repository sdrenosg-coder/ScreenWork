
`KillSignal=SIGTERM` and `TimeoutStopSec=60` give the worker time to finish
the in-flight job (or, more commonly, to return it to the queue) before the
process manager escalates to SIGKILL. The queue's lock TTL (`JOBS_LOCK_TTL_MS`)
covers the case where the worker dies without releasing a lock: the lock
expires and another worker picks the job up.
