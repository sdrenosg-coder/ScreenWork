# Market Guide for Digital Adoption Platforms

Published: 31 August 2026


### Summary

AI is disrupting the digital adoption platform market, causing buyers to question whether to build or buy. Application leaders should use this research to critically evaluate DAPs and prioritize cross-application orchestration including AI to improve efficiency, adoption and work transformation.
