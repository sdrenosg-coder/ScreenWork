
The runner attempts these itself when it has sufficient privileges. If the
application's database role does not have `CREATE EXTENSION` privilege,
run the two statements once as superuser and the runner will skip them.

### 7.3 Retention

The retention policy for artifact and guide rows is configurable per
workspace through the admin UI (`/admin`) and the
`/api/admin/retention` endpoint. The retention sweep deletes expired
artifacts and their object-store contents. The default is 180 days
(`DEFAULT_RETENTION_DAYS`). A workspace's override takes precedence over
the server default.
