
Do not lower timeouts to counteract warm-up; the effect is bounded and
low timeouts will fail real jobs.

---

## 10. Filing an issue

Include:

- `journalctl -u zipy-web -u zipy-worker --since '<timestamp>' --no-pager`
- The release tag (`git -C /opt/zipy describe --tags --always`)
- The failing request as an `curl` command with the cookie redacted.
- The exact HTTP status and response body (never the request body for an
  artifact upload — it may carry customer content).
- Whether the same input succeeds against the operator's staging instance.

Do not paste filled secrets from `/etc/zipy/zipy.env`.
