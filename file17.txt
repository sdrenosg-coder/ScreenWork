
The model choice determines accuracy, memory, and CPU time. On CPU-only
hosts, `small` or `base` are practical; `medium` and `large-v3` are
usable only on a host with a GPU or with the patience for very long
transcription jobs. The platform does not choose a model; it sends
whatever the whisper.cpp server has loaded.
