
The message names the variable. The two hard failures `validateStartupConfig`
enforces are:

- `SESSION_SECRET` missing → real the file at `/etc/zipy/zipy.env`.
- `SESSION_SECRET` shorter than 32 characters → regenerate with
  `openssl rand -hex 32` and restart.

If the journal shows a syntax error in the env file, check for unquoted
spaces or shell metacharacters on the right-hand side; the file is sourced
by systemd, not by a shell, and the parser is stricter.
